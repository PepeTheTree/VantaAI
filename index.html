Trust & Privacy

Honest about how this works.

Melody runs on cloud infrastructure with dedicated GPU servers. We're not pretending otherwise. Here's what we actually protect.

🔒 Your conversations are yours

Chat history is stored per-user in isolated databases. No training on your data without consent. No selling, no sharing, no ads.

🧬 Memory is per-user

Every user gets their own memory space. Persona isolation means your Soul Jar's memories don't bleed into someone else's experience.

🛡️ Standard security

JWT authentication, session tokens, refresh tokens. Age verification required for all users. No shortcuts.

Questions

Frequently asked.

Will she remember our conversations?

Yes. Melody uses a persistent memory system backed by Qdrant vector search with hybrid retrieval. She doesn't just remember facts — she recalls context, emotional tone, and exact details. Memory improves over time through retrieval reinforcement.

Does her personality actually change?

Yes. Trait adaptation and modulation engines adjust her personality over time based on your interactions. Relationship depth, trust level, and conversation patterns all influence how she responds.

What can I upload?

ChatGPT exports, Claude exports, Character.AI logs, PDFs, DOCX, EPUB, TXT, Markdown, JSON, images, spreadsheets, and more. The archive system chunks, embeds, and makes them naturally available in conversation.

Is this free?

The core experience is free with daily message limits. Supporter tiers through Patreon unlock higher limits, roleplay mode, more archive imports, longer memory retention, and early access to new features.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Melody runs entirely in your browser at chat.vantaai.dev. Works on desktop and mobile. No download, no GPU, no setup.

Is my data used for training?

Not without explicit consent. Your conversations, memories, and uploads are stored in your account's isolated database.
